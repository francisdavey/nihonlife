When I came to live in Japan in 2023, everything seemed like a mad rush. There seemed to be many things that needed to be set up before I could comfortably start *living* in Japan.
Not least of these was having a bank account. For reasons that will become clear by the end of this post, that meant an account with the 郵便bank (yucho bank), belonging to Japan's post office, which has been my only Japense banking facility since then.
At that time I was living in the town of Ayagawa in Kagawa Prefecture on the island of Shikoku.

At some point in 2024, I relocated to Amami Oshima. The "relocation" was another mad rush. I had expected to be returning to ayagawa more often and for longer than I did and I had not expected to
become a permanent resident of Amami.
